Privacy policy

Meridian - SEO operations dashboard. Last updated 9 September 2026.

Meridian is an internal tool used by an SEO agency to run search campaigns for its clients. It is not a public service. This page explains what data it holds and what it does with data obtained from Google.

Who uses it

Only the agency's own staff sign in. Clients do not have accounts. Each client's business details, keywords and campaign history are stored so the agency can do the work it was hired for.

Google data

When a client's Google account is connected, Meridian requests two read-only permissions:

  • Search Console (read-only). Search queries, impressions, clicks and average positions for the client's own website. Used to report how the site performs, to suggest topics the site already appears for, and to flag pages that receive no impressions.
  • Google Ads (planning only). Keyword Planner search volumes and competition figures. Used to estimate demand for search phrases. Meridian never creates, edits or runs ads, and never touches billing.

Meridian's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the features above to the agency staff working on that client. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone outside the agency and the client it belongs to.

Storage and security

Access tokens and every other credential are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) and are never shown in the browser after they are saved. Data is held on the agency's own database hosting. Only scoped, revocable credentials are ever stored.

Disconnecting and deletion

A connection can be removed at any time from the client's profile in the dashboard, which deletes the stored tokens immediately. Access can also be revoked from the Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. A client can ask the agency to delete their campaign record entirely.

Contact

Questions and deletion requests go to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app, which is the agency's own address.
