Meridian - SEO operations dashboard. Last updated 9 September 2026.

What this is

Meridian is an internal dashboard operated by an SEO agency for its own staff. Access is by invitation from the agency only. There is no public sign-up and no paid plan.

Use of connected accounts

When a client authorises a Google connection, they grant the agency read-only access to that data for the purpose of running the client's search campaign, as described in the privacy policy. The client may withdraw that access at any time.

Content

Articles, listings and replies drafted in Meridian are reviewed by a person at the agency before they are published anywhere. Nothing is posted to a third-party site without a stored, client-approved credential for that site.

No warranty

The dashboard is provided as-is to the agency's staff. Search rankings, volumes and estimates it shows are informational and depend on third-party data sources.
